## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-11

First public release.

### Added

- SFTP (SSH agent, password or key file, with known_hosts checking) and Amazon
  S3 (AWS profiles, access keys, custom endpoints).
- Bookmarks, tabs with per-tab history, and secrets stored in the system keychain.
- Pipelined listings and transfers, a transfers drawer, conflict handling and
  drag and drop.
